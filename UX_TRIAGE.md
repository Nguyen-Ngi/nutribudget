# NutriBudget UX triage

| Area | Core · stays visible | Supporting · available on demand | Removed from the interface |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home and navigation | One remaining food budget and one next action. Trang chủ, Món ăn, and Mua sắm are the three primary links. | Separate Quản lý menu for Budget and Pantry. Data warnings and completed-period recovery still appear when relevant. | Separate budget strip, repeated countdown and overspending copy, decorative plate motif. |
| Budget | Amount and duration with a seven-day default. Today is the default start date. One remaining balance. | Start date, end preview, carry-over when available, buffer when editing, active-period closing confirmation, manual expenses, and period history. | Onboarding buffer controls, spending bar, and the starting-budget/forecast metric dashboard. |
| Meals | Date and meal slot, individual dish images and estimates, add/remove, search, category, selected count and estimate, save. | Recipe dialog with cooking steps, ingredient, allergen and equipment information. Diet and allergen filters, portions, saved meals, history, and cooking confirmation. | Calendar arrows, date-range panel and slot-status cards, slot toggle, forecast panel, Suggested tab, budget filter, recommendation sliders, and equipment filter. A saved paused meal stays paused until explicitly resumed. |
| Shopping | Comparable sourced merchandise estimates, up to three choices, a single plan action, checklist with retailer and confirmation. | Short price and source details, temporary lists, explicit prices for unknown items, changing an approved list, and purchase history. Price gaps remain visible. | Weekday selections, each retailer's fee inputs, fee checkbox, optimization-mode controls, retailer directory with nested disclosures, standalone ingredient breakdown, and up-front manual product-name and price fields. |
| Pantry | Supporting destination only. Shows current quantities and allows explicit additions and corrections. | Stock, purchase, and cooking history in a disclosure. | Stock and cooked-meal counters and repeated food-group labels. |

Complete plans are comparable only with the same product IDs, package sizes, and package counts for every required ingredient. A unique lowest merchandise cost in a matching group can receive the recommendation. Estimates exclude unconfirmed fees. Legacy recorded charges and any previously approved fee are still accounted for exactly once. The browser storage format and historical purchase records remain compatible.
